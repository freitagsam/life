# One Life — Product Roadmap

## Core promise

A replayable, text-first life simulator where every year creates stories, consequences, relationships, money decisions, and unusual career paths. The interface should feel like a well-made mobile game—not a productivity dashboard.

## Design principles

- Fast to understand and easy to play with one thumb
- Dense with choices without feeling visually crowded
- Warm paper-and-ink visual identity with a few strong colors
- Funny writing grounded in believable consequences
- Original names, artwork, writing, progression, and interface details
- Major careers should contain decisions and gameplay, not just salary changes

## Phase 1 — Playable life loop

- Create or randomize a character
- Age up one year
- Generate age-appropriate events
- Make choices inside event cards
- Update mood, health, smarts, confidence, and money
- Save one life locally on the device
- Birth, childhood, school, adulthood, retirement, and death
- Life summary and legacy score

## Phase 2 — Everyday systems

- Education and qualifications
- Regular jobs, interviews, performance, raises, and termination
- Friends, parents, siblings, dating, marriage, and children
- Cars, homes, possessions, loans, and investments
- Health conditions, exercise, doctors, and habits
- Travel, nightlife, shopping, hobbies, and random encounters
- Crime, police, court, and consequences

## Phase 3 — Playable careers

### Pickleball Pro

- Athletic potential and skill development
- Local leagues, qualifiers, tours, and rankings
- Singles and doubles partners
- Tournament choices and match moments
- Coaches, injuries, rivalries, and retirement
- Prize money, sponsorships, equipment, and reputation

### Influencer

- Platform and content niche selection
- Post creation and performance
- Followers, engagement, reputation, and burnout
- Brand deals, management, collaborations, and merchandise
- Viral moments, controversy, cancellations, and comebacks
- Cross-platform growth and transition into other careers

### Additional career ideas

- Entrepreneur
- Real-estate investor
- Accountant or finance executive
- Professional athlete
- Coach or sports manager
- Actor
- Musician or producer
- Politician
- Lawyer
- Doctor
- Content creator
- Game developer
- Private investigator
- Pastor or ministry worker
- Pilot
- Chef
- Esports competitor

## Phase 4 — Depth and replayability

- Character traits and hidden potential
- Generated people with memories and changing relationships
- City and country differences
- Family generations and inheritance
- Achievements, collections, endings, and challenges
- Multiple saved lives
- Optional cloud account and synchronization
- Seasonal scenarios without making the base game feel incomplete

## Current prototype

- Life profile and four visible stats
- Scrollable life-event timeline
- Working age-up button that generates new events
- Career, assets, relationships, and activity navigation
- Pickleball pro and influencer career previews
- Original visual direction for the game

## Next recommended build

Make age-up open a real choice event with two or three responses, then have the selected response change stats and add a permanent entry to the life log.
